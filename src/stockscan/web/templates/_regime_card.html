How to read this card

Trend gate. SPY above or below its 200-day SMA, with a three-close dwell so a single close through the line does not flip it. Closed → no new longs; open positions run their own exits. The evidence for this filter is drawdown reduction, not return prediction. SPY close {{ "%.2f"|format(regime.spy_close|float) }} · SMA(200) {{ "%.2f"|format(regime.spy_sma200|float) }}.

Vol scalar. Annualized 20-day realized SPY volatility, percentile-ranked over the trailing 252 trading days. When the rank is in the top tercile the scalar is clip(0.16 / realized_vol, 0.5, 1.0), otherwise 1.0. It never scales up. Each strategy declares whether it applies.

Credit stress. ICE BofA US High Yield OAS (FRED BAMLH0A0HYM2). Fires when the spread is in the top 15% of its trailing year and higher than five observations ago; while it fires, new long entries are refused. A breaker only — credit level is not a swing-horizon signal. Refreshed by the nightly macro job.
